Me

I'm a graphic designer with a strong curiosity about the world around me and a particular fascination with what happens beneath the surface.

I love travelling, discovering unfamiliar places and experiencing different ways of seeing and living. I'm drawn to nature in all its contrasts, from forests and mountains to the sea, from cold landscapes to warm ones, from quiet days to nights spent under an open sky. What fascinates me most are the connections between things and how they shape the way we see, feel and experience the world.

That curiosity is at the heart of how I work. What I enjoy most is bringing entire worlds to life. It might be a brand, a concept, an atmosphere, an idea or a feeling that does not yet have a visual form. I like finding what is essential, giving it structure and developing a visual language that makes it tangible.

My foundation is traditional graphic design. I've worked across branding, advertising, visual identities, illustration, print and digital design. More recently, I've discovered a real love for packaging and the challenge of creating a visual world around a physical object. I still value the fundamentals I learned through my experience: concept, hierarchy, typography, composition, detail and the ability to turn an idea into something clear and visually coherent.

At the same time, I'm fascinated by what is possible today. AI has become an exciting part of my creative process. I might bring an idea into AI, explore where it takes it, bring it back into the design process, reshape and refine it, explore it again and eventually bring everything together in Adobe and other tools. I enjoy the dialogue between my own ideas and the possibilities of the technology. The technology is not the idea. It is another way of exploring what the idea could become.

More recently, I've also started building websites myself. Taking an idea from concept and visual direction through structure and design to a functioning published website has opened up another area that I find genuinely exciting.

My career has taken a less conventional path. I spent many years travelling and raising my family, staying creatively engaged along the way through occasional projects and personal work. My portfolio therefore brings together earlier professional work with more recent self initiated projects that reflect where my thinking and interests are today.

I'm now looking for an environment where this combination of experience, visual thinking, curiosity and new ways of working can contribute. If you see potential in my work, I'd love to have a conversation.
